Apply to sell

For artists

Sell your work at an HAA market

We're a juried show: every applicant is reviewed to make sure what's sold is genuinely handmade in Hawai‘i. Here's what it costs, when to apply, and how the process works.

Eligibility

Before you apply, make sure your work fits our jurying criteria.

  • All items must be designed and handmade by the applying artist, in Hawai‘i.
  • No imported, mass-produced, or resold goods — including kits assembled from purchased components.
  • Photography, fine art, and original prints of your own work are welcome.
  • You must be present at your booth for the full event, or send a listed co-vendor.
  • HAA membership is required to hold a booth; day passes are not available.

Booth fees

Fees vary by event size and booth footprint. Electricity and corner booths are add-ons.

HAA is a nonprofit — booth fees cover permits, insurance, marketing, and site costs. Members in good standing get priority placement.

Applications typically close six weeks before each event; payment is due on acceptance.

Late applications are considered only if space remains — email us before submitting past a deadline.

How it works

Submit your application

Fill out the form below with photos of your work and a short description of your process.

Jury review

Our jury reviews submissions within 10 business days to confirm the work is handmade and Hawai‘i-made.

Acceptance & booth fee

Accepted artists receive booth assignment details and pay the booth fee to confirm their spot.

Set up & sell

Arrive at your assigned load-in time, set up your booth, and you're ready for the market.
