FAQ

Questions from our members

If you don't see your question here, reach out — we're happy to help.

What counts as "handmade in Hawai‘i"?

The applying artist must design and make the work themselves, in Hawai‘i. Kits, imported goods, and resold items don't qualify — this is what our jury reviews when you apply.

Do I need to be an HAA member to sell at a market?

Yes. Membership is required to hold a booth. You can apply for membership and a specific event at the same time on our Apply page.

How long does the jury review take?

Typically about 10 business days from when you submit your application. You'll hear from us either way, even if we need more photos of your work.

What happens if I miss the application deadline?

Email us before submitting — we sometimes have room for late applications if space is available, but it isn't guaranteed.

Can I share a booth with another artist?

Yes, our 5×10 shared booth option exists for exactly this. Both artists should be listed on the same application.

Is electricity available at my booth?

At most events, yes, for an additional fee listed on the Apply page. Let us know in your application if you need it, since spots are limited.

What's your refund policy if I can't make an event?

Booth fees are refundable up to two weeks before the event, minus a small processing fee. After that, fees go toward event costs already committed.

How do I get added to the artist directory?

Once you're accepted as a member, we add your listing to the directory with your craft category and a link to your Instagram or portfolio.

Still have a question?

Send us a message and we'll get back to you.
