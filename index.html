Handmade, sold by the hands that made it.

The Handcrafters & Artisans Alliance connects Hawai‘i's makers with the community through juried art fairs, including Waikiki Artfest. Every item is designed and crafted in Hawai‘i by the artist who sells it.

Why HAA exists

Our mission is to give our members a place to sell high-quality, locally made art — and to build real connections with the community and with each other.

Showcase local talent

Every item must be handmade by the artist in Hawai‘i — no resellers, no imports.

Build community

Our events create a supportive space where artists collaborate and inspire each other.

Support the local economy

We promote buying local, which benefits artisans and the small businesses around them.

Enrich our culture

HAA celebrates diverse art forms, adding to the community's cultural landscape.
